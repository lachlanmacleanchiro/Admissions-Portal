NZCC Applicant Portal · Release notes

What changed on 12 October 2026

Staff can now send interview invitations and other letters from reusable templates, with every email checked by a person before it goes out. Settings and applicant records are easier to find your way around, and two privacy and data issues have been fixed.

For: Admissions leadership and staff Prepared: 12 Oct 2026

New

Document templates, such as interview invitations, reviewed and sent by staff.

Your own email account: connect NZCC's email-sending account in Settings.

Fixed

Student files can now only be opened by the student who uploaded them, and by staff.

New features

Document templates for each admissions stage

Live

Admins write letters and emails once and reuse them. Each template belongs to a stage, so the interview invitation appears when an applicant reaches the Interview stage.

  1. Write the template in Settings. A simple editor offers bold, headings, lists and links, plus a live preview.
  2. Personalise it with placeholders. {{preferredName}}, the applicant number and the intake year fill in automatically. Anything else, like {{zoomLink}}, becomes a box staff complete before sending.
  3. Review and send. On the applicant's page, staff open the email, fill in the blanks, adjust the wording if needed and press Send.
  • Nothing sends automatically. Moving an applicant to a new stage never emails them a document. A staff member always reviews and sends it.
  • Every send is on record: who sent it, when, and the exact wording. It also appears on the applicant's timeline and in the audit log.
  • The online interview invitation letter has been set up as the first template.

Email settings: use NZCC's own sending account

Ready

A new Email page in Settings lets an admin enter the sender address, the sender name applicants see, and the access key for NZCC's own Resend account (Resend is the service that delivers the portal's emails). The portal then stops depending on the developer's account.

  • The access key is stored securely and can't be viewed again after saving. Only its last four characters are shown.
  • A Send test email button confirms everything works before applicants rely on it.
  • The on/off switch for automatic notification emails has moved onto this page.

Improvements

Settings split into tabs

Ready

Settings was one long page. It now has four tabs: General (intake years and application errors), Stage Task Templates, Document Templates and Email.

Applicant records are easier to move around

Ready
  • Tabs instead of buttons: Overview, Tasks, Documents, Audit Log and Consideration Summary sit in one tab bar, like Settings.
  • The header stays put. The applicant's name, stage, flag and Status button now look the same on every tab, so staff always know whose record they're in.
  • Uses the full screen. Applicant pages now fill wide monitors instead of stopping halfway across.

Fixes

Student documents are private to that student

Live

Previously, a signed-in student who had another student's file link could open that file. Students can now only open their own documents. Staff access is unchanged.

Deleting a student account works reliably

Live

Deleting an account failed for students who had used message threads or had Year 2 prerequisite courses on file, but the audit log still recorded it as deleted. Deletion now completes, and the log entry is written only once it has.

Known issue

Check the interview time before sending an invitation

Open

The interview panel only stores a date, not a time. For applicants with an interview date entered, the invitation's time placeholder fills in an incorrect time (around midday). Until this is fixed, check the time in the preview and type the correct time into the message before pressing Send. A fix is planned as part of combining interview scheduling with sending the invitation.

What admins need to do

  • Connect NZCC's email account once the Email settings page is released: Settings → Email, then use Send test email.
  • Add the interview invitation template in Settings → Document Templates on the live portal. The example letter was set up for testing and isn't on the live site yet.

Proposed next steps

A full review of the portal found these areas worth prioritising. Each one needs a decision before work starts.

  • One-step interview scheduling: set the date, time and Zoom link once, and the invitation fills itself in. Attendance and reschedules are recorded.
  • Student portal polish: clearer "what to do next" guidance, a single message inbox, a document checklist and a simpler offer page.
  • Privacy: notes staff type when moving an applicant to a new stage currently appear on the student's timeline.
  • Accurate dashboard: some figures on the admin dashboard are placeholders, not real data.
  • Sign-in protection: limit repeated login attempts, as flagged in the July security review.
